Why Streatos

Great products, limited visibility.

A regional product may have strong local demand, traditional methods, a unique taste and a community of experienced producers. It still may never reach a buyer outside its geography.

What the product has

  • Strong local demand
  • Traditional production methods
  • Unique taste and character
  • A long regional history
  • High-quality raw materials

What the producer lacks

  • National marketing
  • Strong packaging
  • Brand building
  • Distribution
  • E-commerce capability

So they stay local products.

Instead of becoming recognised Indian brands. Streatos closes that gap.

How an origin story works

Every pack carries the place it comes from.

Take rajma as one example. Most buyers outside the region know it as a common pulse and have never heard of Bhaderwah as the source of a distinctive one. A Streatos pack closes that gap by carrying the place with it: where it comes from, the local farming practices, the people involved, the regional food traditions and the history behind it. The same model repeats for any product from any region.

The flywheel

Every purchase creates demand for the next producer

Questions

Frequently asked

What is Streatos?

Streatos is a consumer brand and commerce ecosystem that discovers, develops, markets and distributes unique products, foods and everyday goods from Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities, towns, villages and rural regions of India.

What is an Origin Story?

It is the identity a product carries: where it comes from, the region it is produced in, local farming practices, the people involved, regional food traditions and the history tied to it.

How do the restaurants connect to the products?

A customer eats a regional dish, learns about the region, sees the Streatos product used in it, scans the pack for its story and can buy the packaged product to take home.

What will a Streatos Mart sell?

Daily essentials alongside regional and locally sourced products, including pulses, rice, flour, spices, honey, pickles, snacks, dry fruits, beverages, organic and household products, and Streatos private-label products.

Who can partner with Streatos?

Producers such as farmers, cooperatives and small processors, people who want to run a Mart in their town, and businesses such as hotels, caterers and canteens that need regional ingredients.
