About Streatos

We Believe India’s Next Great Products Are Already Being Made Locally.

Across India’s cities, towns and villages, thousands of products are produced with exceptional quality, traditional knowledge and strong regional identity.

The challenge is not always the product.

The challenge is discovery.

A product may be famous in one district but completely unknown in another state.

A local producer may have an excellent product but no access to professional branding, digital commerce or a larger customer base.

Streatos exists to bridge that gap.

What We Do

Streatos is a local-to-national commerce platform connecting regional producers and businesses with consumers.

We source products from local vendors, pack them with the Streatos brand and sell them through our own channels: Streatos Marts, Streatos Restaurants, our e-commerce website, and online platforms such as Instagram and Facebook.

We operate across three major areas:

Marketplace

Online discovery and sale of regional products.

Restaurants

Regional food experiences that introduce consumers to local cuisine and ingredients.

Marts

Physical retail stores combining everyday products with regional specialties.

All of this is supported by our growing network of local Streatos branches.

Why Local Branches Matter

We believe a digital platform alone is not enough for India’s diverse local markets.

Many producers and small businesses need someone locally who can explain the process, help with documentation, assist with technology and provide ongoing support.

Our branches become the local connection between:

  1. People
  2. Products
  3. Businesses
  4. Streatos

A vendor does not have to figure everything out alone.

They can visit their nearest Streatos branch and receive assistance.

Our Mission

To make India’s local products discoverable, accessible and scalable.

We want to help exceptional products move from:

  1. Local Market
  2. Regional Market
  3. National Market

While keeping their origin and identity at the heart of the product.

Our Vision

To build India’s largest ecosystem for discovering and experiencing local products, food and businesses.

We envision a future where consumers can discover a product online, visit the place where it comes from, experience its food, and purchase it from anywhere in India.

What Makes Streatos Different?

We connect commerce with origin.

We don’t want consumers to only know what they are buying.

We want them to know:

Where it comes from.

Who makes it.

Why it is special.

What makes the region unique.

That is the Streatos experience.
