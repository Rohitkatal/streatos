How It Works

  1. Register

    Submit your basic information through the vendor registration form.

  2. Branch Connect

    Our team connects you with the nearest Streatos branch.

  3. Verification

    Required business, vendor and product information is reviewed.

  4. Product Onboarding

    Our team agrees product details, quantity and pricing with you.

  5. Start Supplying

    Once approved, Streatos sources your product, packs it under the Streatos brand and sells it through applicable channels.

  6. Grow

    Reach more customers and explore opportunities to grow your supply.
